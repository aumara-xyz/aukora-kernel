When you trace accountability in any real governance system, you find it flows *downward* readily (citizens are accountable to laws, workers to employers, soldiers to commanders) but encounters topological obstructions flowing *upward*. Power creates sinks, nodes where accountability flows in but never flows out. These sinks are invariant across regime type. A king, a CEO, a party chairman, a supreme court are different names with the same topological role: an accountability sink.
