## The gap
Kira atoms/receipts are hash-chained (tamper-evident) but not recoverability-checked. An atom derived from an MDL (multi-document/long-context) source has no replay provenance recorded — there's no way to re-derive it from its source and confirm it still matches. If a source mutates or a derivation step was buggy, the atom silently drifts from ground truth with no detection mechanism.

## Proposed shape (not yet designed in detail)
- Atoms carry a `replayProvenance` field: enough to regenerate the atom's text deterministically from its stated source (source path/ref + as_of + the transform applied).
- A `kira.sh verify-replay <atomId>` (or folded into the existing `verify` command) that re-runs the derivation and compares against the stored atom text.
- On mismatch: quarantine at recall time (the atom stops surfacing in `recall()` results) rather than either silently trusting stale content or hard-failing the whole brain.

## Explicitly out of scope for this issue
- No change to the hash-chain/receipt mechanism itself (SAFETY_LAWS 7 already covers tamper-evidence).
- Not a retroactive backfill of replay provenance for the current 76 atoms — v1 scope is new ingests only, existing atoms stay as-is.
