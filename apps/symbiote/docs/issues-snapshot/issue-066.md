Why this exists:
The useful part of the donor archive is not its code; it is the idea that game/puzzle wins produce reusable reasoning principles for Fusion. Fusion should eventually track the shape of reasoning, not only final votes.

Good principles to preserve:
- Visited hypothesis tracking: hash normalized hypotheses/reasoning paths and avoid re-exploring duplicates.
- Winner streak limits: detect if one model/framework dominates repeatedly and require contrarian review.
- Dead-end detection: when repeated attempts fail or most votes reject/abstain, change strategy instead of spending more rounds.
- Fog/exploration bias: when coherence is low or uncertainty is high, deliberately broaden exploration rather than forcing premature consensus.
- Converge-then-freeze: when a bounded review reaches stable evidence, stop spending tokens.
- Deterministic seeding: comparable problem inputs should produce comparable council framing so regressions are visible.
