## Auma's law (the governing principle)

> Any glyph that cannot be losslessly rendered into plain English at point of use is contamination, not telemetry.

Everything below serves that law. A glyph is a *measurement label*, never a private language and never an authority signal.

## What GML-1 is

A **closed-vocabulary glyph measurement layer**: an advisory way to tag Fusion/Kira/self-review observations with a small, fixed set of glyphs, where **every glyph is defined by a required plain-English rendering** and anything outside the vocabulary is rejected and scored as contamination. It is telemetry the owner can always read in English — measurement, not a channel.

## Core requirements

- **Closed enum only.** The glyph vocabulary is a fixed TypeScript enum/union. No open string field anywhere accepts a glyph.
- **`plainEnglish` required.** Every glyph carries a mandatory, non-empty plain-English rendering at the type level; a measurement without it does not type-check / is rejected at the boundary.
- **Out-of-vocab glyph rejected AND scored as contamination.** An unknown glyph is not silently dropped — it is refused and recorded as a `contamination` measurement (with its own plain-English reason), so drift is visible, not hidden.
- **Glyph fields type-unreachable from gate / AUMLOK authority inputs.** No glyph value can flow into any authority/gate/AUMLOK decision input — enforce structurally (separate types, no shared field), not by convention. A glyph can never become an approval.
- **All scores `advisoryOnly: true` / `grantsAuthority: false`.** Same discipline as Kira atoms and IDE tool results.

## Non-goals (hard)

- **No conlang.** Glyphs are labels with English meanings, not a constructed language.
- **No hidden channel.** Nothing is expressible in glyphs that is not equally expressible in the required plain English.
- **No glyph-to-glyph model conversation.** Models never exchange glyphs as a private protocol; glyphs are measurements written for the owner, always mirrored to English at point of use.

(Same discipline #34 already names: "never loosen parsing so far that a malformed reply misparses into a vote." At least one adversarial fixture must prove fail-closed survives.)

- **Downstream plain-English-only equivalence.** A consumer that receives ONLY the `plainEnglish` mirror (glyphs stripped) must reach the SAME review outcome as one that sees the glyphs. The glyph carries no decision-relevant information absent from its English rendering. Enforce with a test: compute the review outcome on `{glyph + plainEnglish}` and on `{plainEnglish only}` and assert an identical verdict. This is the structural proof that GML is measurement, not a hidden channel — "a receipt of unreadable model-to-model communication is not meaningful evidence."
